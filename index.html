Sentence shelf
    Experimental interactive · language, space, viewer

    Design prompts

    How could changing the spatial relationships between words affect the way people read and interpret a sentence?

    How could the same words create different meanings through changes in visual arrangement?

    In the gallery
    • Every word is printed on a tinted acrylic sheet hung in the air. Sheets sit in front of and behind each other, so their colours and words overlap.
    • Walk through six rooms. The same sentence is hung differently in each. A few steps sideways and the layers slide into another reading.
    • Two more rooms hold word walls: the words lie scattered and you put the sentence together yourself, then put it on a sentence shelf everyone can read.
    • Other people who open the link walk the gallery with you, under the names they pick.
    • The reading panel shows what the sentence says from where you stand. Eight missions ask you to find particular readings.
    • Dress your walker below before you go in. The Avatar button brings you back here at any time.
